How This Tracker Works

  • 🎱 Current player is highlighted with a green border
  • 🔴 Pot reds (1 pt) then any color, which gets re-spotted
  • 🎯 After all 15 reds, pot colors in order: Yellow→Green→Brown→Blue→Pink→Black
  • ⚠️ Only valid shots are clickable (others are grayed out)
  • 💥 Fouls give variable points: Yellow/Green/Brown(4), Blue(5), Pink(6), Black(7)
  • 🏆 Game ends when all balls potted or win is mathematically certain
  • 📊 Break tracking shows consecutive scoring for current player
